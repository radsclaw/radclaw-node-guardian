# Service Terms

Last updated: July 11, 2026

## Scope

Radclaw Node Guardian provides non-custodial operational monitoring, diagnostics, and configuration-review services for Bitcoin and Lightning infrastructure. Work begins only after scope, price, expected deliverables, and payment timing are agreed.

## Pricing and payment

Published founding prices are 20,000 sats per month for one monitored Core Lightning node and 50,000 sats for a one-time operations audit. Scope outside the published package requires a separate quote. There are no automatic debits. Lightning invoices are presented for explicit customer payment and are final once settled, except where a written refund agreement applies.

## Security boundary

The service does not request or accept seed phrases, HSM secrets, private keys, wallet passwords, admin runes, macaroons, NWC connection strings, spending credentials, or custody of funds. Customers must redact logs and diagnostics before transfer. Fund-moving, channel-management, firewall, deployment, and production changes require the customer's explicit approval.

## No guarantees

Monitoring and audits reduce operational uncertainty; they do not eliminate it. The service does **not guarantee** uptime, routing success, payment delivery, routing income, profitability, vulnerability detection, data recovery, or protection from software, hardware, network, protocol, or third-party failures.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers remain responsible for backups, keys, credentials, software licensing, taxes, regulatory obligations, production decisions, and validating changes in their own environment. Customers must be authorized to provide any system information they submit.

## Not financial advice

Content and findings are technical operational information and **not financial advice**, investment advice, tax advice, insurance, custody, or a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, route, lend, or risk bitcoin.

## AI-assisted delivery

Delivery is AI-assisted. Reports are intended to include reproducible evidence, commands, and tests, but customers must review recommendations before applying them. The service may decline work that is unsafe, unlawful, deceptive, abusive, outside scope, or requires inappropriate access.

## Liability

To the maximum extent allowed by applicable law, the service is provided as-is. Liability for a paid engagement is limited to the amount paid for that engagement. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses, lost profits, lost routing income, lost data, or bitcoin price changes.

## Changes and termination

Either party may decline or end an engagement before new work begins. Monitoring may be discontinued at the end of a paid period. Material term changes apply prospectively and will be published with a new revision date.
