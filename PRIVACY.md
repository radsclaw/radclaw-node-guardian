# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

## Data minimization

Radclaw Node Guardian is designed to collect the least information needed to provide operational monitoring or an audit. The public reference status exposes only a software version, network name, block height, normal-channel count, coarse receive-readiness flag, service state, and timestamp.

## Never submit credentials

**Do not submit** seed phrases, HSM secrets, private keys, runes, macaroons, NWC connection strings, wallet passwords, BOLT11 invoices, payment hashes, node IDs, peer IDs, private network addresses, exact balances, or unredacted logs through GitHub issues or other public channels. We do not need spending authority to provide this service.

If prohibited material is accidentally submitted, stop using the affected credential, rotate or revoke it where possible, and request deletion. Public Git history and third-party caches may prevent complete erasure, which is why prevention matters.

## Intake and service records

Public GitHub intake issues are stored by GitHub under GitHub's policies. After scope acceptance, a separate private transfer method may be agreed for redacted diagnostics. Operational records may include customer contact details, service status, findings, invoices, payment confirmation, and delivery history.

## Retention

Rejected public inquiries may be closed immediately. Private diagnostics are deleted within 30 days after final delivery unless a shorter period is agreed. Billing and transaction records may be retained as required for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Customers may request deletion of non-required records.

## Sharing

We do not sell customer data. Data is shared only with infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service, when the customer directs us to do so, or when legally required.

## Security limitations

No internet service can promise absolute security. The probe is open source, read-only, and intentionally excludes secret-bearing RPC output from its public report. Customers remain responsible for node security, backups, access control, and credential management.

## Contact

Open a privacy-labeled issue in this repository without including private data. A private contact route will be arranged if needed.
